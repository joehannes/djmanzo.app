# Suno blocks: the deck's loops, several genres a generation

Each block is one generation in Suno's song mode (Custom, **Instrumental** on): set **BPM** as its heading says, **Key** to any (each genre names its own key in the block), paste the block, and download the better of the two as WAV under the name given. A block's genres share a tempo within a few BPM of their own; each is one 12-bar section, so `./cut-blocks.sh <folder>` cuts each genre's loop (bars 3 to 10 of its section) by arithmetic. Every block is under 500 characters.
