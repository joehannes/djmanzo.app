# Suno Sounds: the deck's loops, one genre per loop

Create → Custom → **Sounds** → **Loop** (Pro or Premier). Per genre: set **BPM** and **Key** as its heading says (not auto, not any), paste its block (the tempo and key are said again in it, as Suno does not always keep to the settings), create, and download the better of the two as WAV under the name given. `./cut-sounds.sh <folder>` then trims each to whole bars. Every block is under 500 characters. Genres marked NEW are not on the site yet.
