# Make the island small, playable, honest

Version 3 of the djmanzo website should be **one small, finishable world in which each station has a single verb**: play a deck, sing a line, dig a crate. Sound should guide visitors more than arrows or modals do, the first touch of any toy should sound good, and every task (download, shop, forum, wishlist) should also have a **plain HTML route that never passes through the spectacle**. That is the pattern behind the strongest 2025–26 award winners (abeto's *Messenger* and *Igloo Inc.*, Bruno Simon's 2025 island). The criticism those sites drew, which was long loads, a CPU pinned at 100%, canvas-only inaccessibility and "terrible as an actual home page", is the precise list of failures to avoid. On technology, the right move is to keep and enhance the existing Svelte 5 + three.js r186 + custom painterly WebGL pipeline. Add **GSAP 3.15, which is now entirely free including SplitText, MorphSVG and ScrollSmoother**, Blender-baked camera paths, Rive for a guide character, and a stem scheduler on the existing Web Audio engine. Switch to WebGPU only after the GLSL painter has been ported to TSL, and skip Theatre.js, which has not published since May 2024. On content, there is a clear gap the site can own: **no well-known interactive web explainer exists for the Camelot wheel, beatmatching by ear, phrasing or EQ swaps**. Assets can be made almost free and licence-clean through Cloudflare Workers AI and Apache-2.0 image models. Hunyuan models are barred in the EU, and Suno music is commercially usable only if it was downloaded during a paid month. Community features should borrow from Blender Extensions, Discourse and Club Penguin. Special offers must obey EU rules that already ban fake countdowns and require the 30-day lowest price beside any discount.

## Award winners share one verb, one small world and an ending

The 2025–26 sites with the most influence are small, bounded worlds. *Messenger* (Awwwards Site of the Day, 10 November 2025) is a "15-minute experimental" browser game in which you deliver mail on a tiny planet you can walk all the way round. It has **no tutorials and "no giant arrows"**. A guitar-playing character gets louder in 3D space as you approach, so sound does the wayfinding ([Awwwards case study](https://www.awwwards.com/messenger.html)). Its social layer is capped by design: **10 players per room, because more "felt too crowded", and 3D emoji instead of text chat** to avoid moderation problems. The team's proudest result was non-gamer relatives playing it without instructions ([Awwwards case study](https://www.awwwards.com/messenger.html)). Bruno Simon's December 2025 portfolio, a drive-a-car island, follows the same restraint. It allows **30 short "whispers" at once, moderated with a free moderation model**, plus shared weather and a daily top-10 leaderboard. A fully shared physics world was dropped because of server cost and griefing risk ([Awwwards case study](https://www.awwwards.com/brunos-portfolio-case-study.html)). *Igloo Inc.* (abeto with Bureaux, Awwwards Site of the Year and Developer Site of the Year, confirmed March 2025) shows its portfolio companies as unique objects frozen in ice across **three scroll sections**. Music and sound effects are synced to its particles, and the intro is rendered live rather than pre-rendered, to save weight ([Awwwards case study](https://www.awwwards.com/igloo-inc-case-study.html); [abeto on X](https://x.com/abeto_co/status/1900152588768579701)). The 2025 Site of the Year, OFF+BRAND's Lando Norris site, worked as "personality in motion" and was built in under two months. Its launch coincided with a title win that "probably helped the overall impact", a reminder that awards also measure timing ([OFF+BRAND](https://www.itsoffbrand.com/our-work/lando-norris); [Codrops](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/08/17/creativity-at-enterprise-scale-without-compromise-the-offbrand-story/)).

The music toys that have lasted add a second rule: **the visitor cannot make a bad sound**. In Blob Opera, "pitches stick to a scale, so there are no wrong notes" ([Google Experiments](https://experiments.withgoogle.com/blob-opera)). In Incredibox the characters *are* the instruments: you drag a sound onto a beatboxer, and loops pre-synced to tempo "make you sound good before you understand why". The company claims over 100 million players ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incredibox); [Webiano](https://webiano.digital/incredibox-makes-beatmaking-feel-like-play/)). Ableton's Learning Synths lets experts **skip the lessons and jump to a playground** ([Ableton blog](https://www.ableton.com/en/blog/learn-synthesis-in-your-browser/)). Radio Garden shows that a single spatial metaphor can be the entire navigation when the metaphor *is* the function: the globe is a radio dial ([Radio World](https://www.radioworld.com/global/radio-garden-turns-earth-into-radio-tuner)). Spotify Wrapped shows how use becomes distribution. It copied the social-story format, reached **120 million engaged users in 2022**, and was criticised in 2024 for being less fun and less accurate ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spotify_Wrapped); [UX Collective](https://uxdesign.cc/whats-happened-to-spotify-wrapped-cdfc32bf56a7)). For djmanzo these map directly onto the existing island:

- The demo deck is beat-synced and key-locked by default, so the first crossfade always sounds good.
- A companion character *is* a deck or a mic rather than a narrator.
- The booths leak music spatially, so sound pulls visitors toward features.
- "Your first mix" and "your karaoke night" recaps can be shared.

### What failed, and the evidence on guidance and stores

The failures are just as consistent. Hacker News readers of Bruno Simon's 2025 site reported:

- a long load with **"no load progress indicator"**, and people closing the tab before seeing anything;
- a continuous render loop holding the CPU near 100%;
- blank first loads on some browsers;
- iOS long-press selecting the whole screen.

Several called it "terrible as an actual home page" ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46206531)). *Messenger*'s lowest jury score was **Usability (7.46)**, and its accessibility score was 7.60 ([Awwwards SOTD](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/messenger)). NN/g's scrolljacking study found that most participants were disoriented. Task-oriented users felt their time was wasted, text placed inside a scrolljack caused the worst problems, and mobile made everything worse. Its bottom line was "more pain than benefit" ([NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/scrolljacking-101/)). WCAG adds that reduced motion does not excuse autoplay: anything that loops still needs a pause control, so a skip-intro button is mandatory ([Passiro](https://passiro.com/web-accessibility/designers/animation-motion)). For guidance, the documented wins come from **one self-selection question, not mascots**. Hotjar asked about expertise and reported **+26% installations** among users who answered, and Crowd Content reported +15% from a choose-your-path modal; both figures are vendor case studies ([CaseStudies.com](https://www.casestudies.com/company/appcues/case-study/hotjar-increases-installations-by-26-with-appcues); [Appcues](https://appcues.com/examples/crowd-content-choose-your-own-adventure-onboarding)). The djmanzo companion should therefore ask at most one question ("DJ, karaoke host, or just curious?"), change the route rather than add steps, and never return uninvited.

Evidence that a spatial "store as a place" works is thin. The 3D shops found (DeepSee Commerce, KidSuper World, WooCommerce WebGL showroom plugins) either name mid-range performance as their main pain or publish no data, and no A/B comparison against grids was found ([hontran.dev](https://www.hontran.dev/blog/deepsee-commerce-3d-ecommerce-website-case-study); [Creativepool](https://creativepool.com/basementstudio/projects/kidsuper-world-for-kidsuper/who-liked)). The well-documented pattern is the **store as an event**: Steam's themed sales that games opt into, and Playdate's "season" of 12 games delivered over time ([Steamworks](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/promo/themed_sales?l=koreana); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/05/22/panics-playmate-is-a-pint-sized-gaming-machine-with-a-season-of-12-intriguing-titles)). The defensible design is a crate-digging booth on the island that shows the week's featured items, as the entrance to a plain, filterable grid. To show the desktop app "in the hand", Apple's technique is cheaper and sturdier than live 3D: a scroll-scrubbed `<canvas>` flipbook of roughly 60–180 pre-rendered frames. One developer cut a hero sequence from about 21 MB to about 4 MB by moving from PNG to WebP ([CSS-Tricks](https://css-tricks.com/?p=308477); [GSAP forum](https://gsap.com/community/forums/topic/25188-airpods-image-sequence-animation-using-scrolltrigger/)). These notes could not source the Teenage Engineering, Nothing, Linear, Raycast, Spline-made, Nintendo eShop or DJ-vendor sites, so they are absent here rather than described from memory.

## Keep the WebGL painter, add GSAP, and treat WebGPU as phase two

The current site already runs Svelte 5 and three.js r186 on `WebGLRenderer`. Its painterly full-screen pass is a GLSL `ShaderMaterial` (`website/src/signal/world/painter.ts`), and it has its own Web Audio engine. Those facts decide most of the stack. **three.js r186 (0.186.1, 24 September 2026)** recommends `WebGPURenderer` with TSL and automatic WebGL 2 fallback for new work ([npm](https://registry.npmjs.org/three); [GitHub releases](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/releases)). However, `WebGPURenderer` **supports no `ShaderMaterial`, `onBeforeCompile`, `EffectComposer` or pmndrs `postprocessing` on either backend**. The brand's painter must therefore be ported to a TSL `RenderPipeline` before any switch ([Utsubo migration guide](https://www.utsubo.com/blog/webgpu-threejs-migration-guide)). The payoff is not guaranteed: WebGPU helps most with many draw calls and compute, and one forum case found it slower than WebGL ([Utsubo 2026](https://www.utsubo.com/blog/threejs-2026-what-changed)). WebGPU is also **not Baseline**. It ships in Chrome, Edge, Chrome Android and Safari/iOS 26, but Firefox has it only on Windows and Apple Silicon and Firefox Android not at all ([caniuse](https://caniuse.com/webgpu); [webstatus.dev](https://webstatus.dev/features/webgpu)). A widely shared claim that WebGPU "hit Baseline in every major browser" is contradicted by that data ([vr.org](https://vr.org/articles/webgpu-baseline-2026-three-js-webxr-default)). The sane plan is to launch v3 on WebGL and port the painter to TSL in a branch. r184's compatibility layer runs TSL node materials on `WebGLRenderer` too. Then screenshot-diff the port against the current build under Xvfb and switch renderers only once the two match.

The rejected options are rejected for specific reasons. **Ready Player Me's public service went offline on 31 January 2026** after Netflix acquired it ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/netflix-acquires-avatar-creation-platform-ready-player-me)). Needle Engine needs paid per-seat Pro licences for any commercial work ([Needle EULA](https://needle.tools/eula)). Mixamo is royalty-free but described as being in maintenance mode, and it forbids redistributing raw files ([Adobe community FAQ](https://community.adobe.com/t5/mixamo-discussions/mixamo-faq-licensing-royalties-ownership-eula-and-tos/m-p/13234775)). Babylon.js and PlayCanvas are healthy, but adopting either would discard the custom engine, which contradicts the directive to enhance rather than rewrite.

On audio, the **Web Audio API and AudioWorklet have been Baseline since 2021**, so the existing engine is a sound base ([webstatus.dev](https://webstatus.dev/features/web-audio)). Tone.js's stable tag dates from April 2025 even though prereleases continue. Howler (2023) and Meyda (2024) are dormant ([npm: tone](https://registry.npmjs.org/tone); [npm: howler](https://registry.npmjs.org/howler)). Adaptive music needs only a small scheduler: decode all stems, start them together at a shared `currentTime`, and ramp gains per scene for vertical layering ([bugnet.io](https://bugnet.io/blog/adaptive-music-a-beginners-guide)). Rive's own audio is meant for "shorter sounds", so keep it to UI clicks ([Rive docs](https://rive.app/docs/editor/events/audio-events)). Browsers block audible Web Audio until a user gesture, and Firefox blocks Web Audio by default ([MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/Media/Guides/Autoplay)). That makes a deliberate "enter with sound" gate both compliant and a good first beat of the guided journey.

Performance practice is settled:

- Compress geometry with **glTF-Transform and meshopt**, which preserves animation and morph targets better than Draco ([svilenkovic](https://svilenkovic.com/3d/meshopt-compression)).
- Encode textures as **KTX2, capped at 1024–2048 px, or 512–1024 px on mobile**, because textures are usually 80%+ of file size ([Cinevva](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/optimize-glb-for-web)).
- Load assets per scene.
- Branch on `prefers-reduced-motion` to replace camera flights with cuts.
- Add a frame-time governor alongside the existing `quality.ts` tier guess, because `detect-gpu`'s lookup data stopped updating in February 2025 ([npm](https://registry.npmjs.org/detect-gpu)).

Bruno Simon's own rationale states the stakes: a 10 FPS experience loses visitors ([Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/brunos-portfolio-case-study.html)).

## Teach by touching: the Camelot wheel and beatmatching are unclaimed ground

The best music explainers hand you a working instrument before any explanation, change one control at a time, use real songs as worked examples, and end in a playground. Ableton Learning Music takes apart "Single Ladies" and Robert Hood's "Ride" and ends in a playground that exports to Live ([FACT](https://factmag.com/2017/05/08/ableton-learning-music-website)). Its July 2026 successor, **Ableton Learn**, swaps video for simplified, mobile-first interactive versions of Live's own tools ([CDM](https://cdm.link/2026/07/ableton-learn/)). Chrome Music Lab's Song Maker shares songs by link, and its Spectrogram makes frequencies visible ([Tech & Learning](https://www.techlearning.com/news/learning-music-art-science-math-and-more-with-chrome-music-lab)). Fuser's colour-coded stems are introduced one at a time. It was criticised for popping up instructions while the music kept demanding attention ([Vooks](https://vooks.net/fuser-switch-review); [Prima Games](https://primagames.com/featured/fuser-harmonix-game-review)). That gives a firm rule: **pause, loop or quieten the music whenever text is on screen**.

DJ-specific teaching on the web is still mostly static. Mixed In Key explains the Camelot wheel with images and rules: from 8A you mix into 8A, 7A, 9A or 8B ([Mixed In Key](https://mixedinkey.com/workflows/how-to-use-the-camelot-wheel/)). The notes found no widely cited interactive Camelot toy, and no web explainer for beatmatching by ear, phrase alignment or EQ bass swaps. That is an inference from search results, not a proven absence, but it is djmanzo's opening. The strongest candidates are:

- **a playable Camelot dial**: tap 8A and hear its neighbours light up, drag a second key to hear consonance or clash, and credit Mark Davis and Mixed In Key;
- **"nudge the platter"**: drag a pitch fader until two galloping loops lock;
- **"count the phrase"**: drop a mix-in marker on the real beat grid and hear a mid-phrase entry clash;
- **"un-mix it"**: colour-coded stems, with a "make it karaoke" button that mutes the vocals and links the theory directly to the karaoke feature.

Standard theory claims that the notes did not source must be checked before publishing. These include the A/B minor–major convention, perfect fifths around the wheel, 8/16/32-bar phrases, and roughly a semitone of key shift per ±6% of pitch.

Progress should be a **skill tree of real DJ skills, never a daily streak**. Smashing Magazine's 2026 review traces streaks' grip to loss aversion: losses feel about twice as strong as gains. It recommends freezes, grace windows, decay instead of reset, and copy that "remind[s] users of how far they have come, not threaten[s] them" ([Smashing Magazine](https://smashingmagazine.com/2026/02/designing-streak-system-ux-psychology/)). "Streak creep" and Duolingo burnout are documented ([The Decision Lab](https://thedecisionlab.com/insights/consumer-insights/streak-creep-the-perils-of-too-much-gamification)). Yousician's critique also applies to DJ software, because a microphone "hears the result, not the technique" ([Violet Recording](https://violetrecording.com/yousician-review/)). Software can grade beat alignment and key compatibility, but it cannot grade taste or crowd-reading, and achievements should never claim to.

### How DJ and karaoke brands sell, and what to do differently

Every competitor's free tier has a gate:

- **VirtualDJ** is free only for home use without a controller ([DJ.Studio](https://dj.studio/blog/budget-dj-software-beginners-amateur)).
- **Serato DJ Lite** works with Lite controllers ([DJ.Studio](https://dj.studio/blog/amateur-dj-software-affordable-beginner-friendly)).
- **rekordbox Free** mainly prepares users for club-standard AlphaTheta gear ([DJ.Studio](https://dj.studio/blog/amateur-dj-software-affordable-beginner-friendly)).
- **djay** keeps Neural Mix stem separation behind PRO, needs an A12-class chip or newer on iOS, and does not separate streamed music ([Algoriddim help](https://help.algoriddim.com/topic/first-steps/free-vs-pro)).

djmanzo's credible difference is stems, a teaching assistant and karaoke hosting in one app. The site should *show* these working and state the real free-tier gates plainly. Education is moving toward community partnerships. AlphaTheta's August 2026 course with FEMME HOUSE runs four sessions at **pay-what-you-can** and covers phrasing, beatmatching and energy control ([Interspace Music](https://interspacemusic.com/blog/alphatheta-and-femme-house-launch-intro-to-djing-online-course/)). It is a wholesome model for any djmanzo "learn" programme. The visual counter to neon-and-laser DJ marketing is Teenage Engineering's: a frame limited to black, white and three greys, where "the products supply the color" ([shadcn.io write-up](https://www.shadcn.io/design/teenage-engineering/raw)). This suits a painterly island in which the interactive parts carry the colour.

Karaoke brands sell the party rather than the software. KaraFun advertises about **59,000 songs**, guests controlling it from their phones, photos shown under the lyrics, and offline play, with a $49/month venue tier ([KaraFun](https://karafun.com/subscribe.html)). Singa leads with original-artist recordings and a 30-day trial ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id802874608)). Catalogue size is the industry's headline number. If djmanzo karaoke runs on the user's own library plus stem separation, the site must say so rather than imply a licensed catalogue. Japan offers a gentler frame than the stage. Private karaoke boxes, and now solo "hitokara" venues such as 1Kara, make singing intimate and low-pressure ([Tofugu](https://www.tofugu.com/travel/1kara-solo-karaoke/)). That argues for marketing a "private room" or solo-practice mode. Origin stories can be told accurately: Shigeichi Negishi's 1967 Sparko Box, and Daisuke Inoue's unpatented 8 Juke, which won him a 2004 Ig Nobel ([Tokyo Weekender](https://www.tokyoweekender.com/entertainment/tech-trends/history-of-karaoke-in-japan/)).

### Real-life culture worth connecting to

The two landmark genre maps are frozen. **Every Noise at Once stopped updating after Spotify's December 2023 layoffs**, at about 6,291 genres ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/02/12/every-noise-shut-down-spotify-layoffs)), and **Ishkur's Guide v3 dates from 2019** ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ishkur%27s_Guide_to_Electronic_Music)). That leaves room for a small, living, sourced genre map that honours both by name. Lineages are contested, which is exactly why each step needs a citation. Dembow's name comes from Shabba Ranks' 1990 "Dem Bow". Its most-sampled loop came from Jamaican and Panamanian collaborators at Philip Smart's HC&F studio on Long Island, and it reached Puerto Rico before the later Dominican scene ([Red Bull Music Academy](https://daily.redbullmusicacademy.com/2013/07/dembow-a-loop-history); [Wikipedia](https://wikipedia.com/wiki/Dem_Bow)). The original lyrics are anti-gay, so a feature should celebrate the riddim, not the song. For wholesome real-life touchpoints, Daybreaker's sober morning dance parties report more than 500,000 participants in about 30 cities ([Freethink](https://www.freethink.com/entrepreneurship-innovation/sober-morning-raves?amp=1)). Together with community DJ courses, dance classes and karaoke nights, they give the site a "good parts of the scene" panel to replace bottle-service and festival-crowd imagery. Lineages of house, techno, hip-hop, bachata, merengue and electro swing were not researched with sources and need their own pass before any of them appears on the site.

## Make every asset almost free and licence-clean

The leading open-weight image models in October 2026 sit roughly 160 Elo behind the best closed model. On Artificial Analysis's open-weights board, Qwen-Image-2.1 leads at 1042, ahead of Ideogram 4.0, FLUX.2 [dev] at 1000, NVIDIA Cosmos3 and HunyuanImage 3.0 ([Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/image/leaderboard/text-to-image?open-weights=true)). The ranking matters less than the licence. **Qwen-Image-2512 (20B) is Apache-2.0** ([HF card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen-Image-2512)), as are **Z-Image Turbo (6B)** ([Qubrid](https://qubrid.com/models/z-image-turbo-lora)) and **FLUX.2 [klein] 4B** ([HF card](https://huggingface.co/black-forest-labs/FLUX.2-klein-4B)). FLUX.2 [dev]'s weights are non-commercial, yet its card states that "generated outputs can be used for personal, scientific, and commercial purposes" ([HF card](https://huggingface.co/black-forest-labs/FLUX.2-dev)). The site's Impressum implies an EU operator, and **Tencent's Hunyuan licences exclude the EU, UK and South Korea**, which rules out HunyuanImage and Hunyuan3D entirely ([Hunyuan3D-2 licence](https://huggingface.co/tencent/Hunyuan3D-2/blob/main/LICENSE)). Qwen-Image-2.1's own licence is reported as non-commercial "Qwen Research" by one secondary source and is unverified ([Pinggy](https://pinggy.io/blog/best_free_open_source_ai_image_generators_to_self_host/)). Free consumer tiers are a weak basis for commercial art. **Leonardo keeps perpetual rights to public images, and free generations cannot be private** ([Leonardo help](https://intercom.help/leonardo-ai/en/articles/8044018-commercial-usage)). Sources contradict each other on free commercial use for Ideogram, Firefly, Bing and Krea, so each must be verified before use.

Compute can be nearly free. **Cloudflare Workers AI, which djmanzo already uses, gives 10,000 neurons a day** ([Cloudflare pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/platform/pricing/)). Converting that price table at 1024×1024 gives:

| Workers AI model | Free images/day | Cost per 1,000 beyond the free allowance |
|---|---|---|
| flux-1-schnell (4 steps) | ≈170 | ≈$0.63 |
| flux-2-klein-4b | ≈95 | ≈$1.15 |
| flux-2-klein-9b | ≈7 | ≈$15 |
| flux-2-dev (20 steps) | ≈3 | ≈$33 |
| leonardo/phoenix-1.0 | ≈4 | ≈$26 |

The arithmetic is the notes' own, from Cloudflare's per-tile and per-step prices. Leonardo-class quality is therefore available **without a Leonardo account**. Whether Cloudflare's agreement with BFL passes commercial output rights through for [dev] was not checked. For heavier work:

- **Kaggle** gives roughly 30 GPU-hours a week on 2×T4 or a P100, with sessions up to 12 hours ([gpuperhour.com](https://gpuperhour.com/blog/free-cloud-gpus-and-credits)).
- **Hugging Face ZeroGPU** gives free accounts 5 minutes a day and PRO 40 minutes a day, on 48 GB Blackwell slices ([HF docs](https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/spaces-zerogpu)).
- **Colab's free tier** still blocks remote web UIs such as ComfyUI front ends ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/197428/google-colab-stable-diffusion-web-ui-ban)).

Consistency comes from a **style LoRA trained on base Z-Image rather than Turbo**, because training on Turbo degrades the distillation ([Civitai docs](https://developer.civitai.com/orchestration/recipes/training-other-image)). Train it on 20–30 hand-picked images generated from the existing art bible's prompt prefix. For upscaling, Real-ESRGAN's code is BSD-3, while **SUPIR is non-commercial** ([HF SUPIR licence](https://huggingface.co/spaces/Upscaler/SUPIR/blob/fae2f4559b8dc6e2ae95973704b7124745162cd7/LICENSE)). For 3D props, **Stable Fast 3D** outputs GLB directly and is free below US$1M annual revenue, including its outputs ([HF card](https://huggingface.co/stabilityai/stable-fast-3d)). TRELLIS.2 is MIT itself but depends on research-only NVIDIA rasterisers, so keep it for prototypes ([TRELLIS issue #298](https://github.com/microsoft/TRELLIS/issues/298)). A per-asset provenance manifest (model, licence, seed, date, plan) extends the repository's existing licence discipline to art.

**Suno's terms effective 3 September 2026 grant commercial use only for songs downloaded while on Pro or Premier.** Suno assigns its rights to the subscriber but "makes no representation or warranty to you that any copyright will vest in any Output". Remixes of other users' songs stay non-commercial, and watermarks and metadata must not be stripped ([Suno ToS](https://suno.com/terms-of-service)). Downloads are now capped: **Free gets 7 lifetime downloads for non-commercial use, Pro 20 a month and Premier 60 a month**. Commercial rights survive cancellation ([Suno help](https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13614785)). In practice, buy one Pro month for production, draft and audition on the platform, download only the finals, and log the plan and date of each. Purely AI tracks may not be protectable, so others could reuse them and Content ID claims are possible. Udio is no source at all: it disabled downloads after its October 2025 Universal settlement and became a walled garden ([Billboard](https://billboard.com/pro/udio-deal-backlash-ai-users-download-ai-songs-48-hours); [RouteNote](https://routenote.com/blog/udio-stops-user-downloads-after-umg-deal-heres-why/)).

## Build the community like Blender, Discourse and Club Penguin, not like a sale

For a **free** marketplace of skins, themes and visuals with donation links, Blender Extensions is the closest precedent. Its 2022 proposal allowed external funding links but no paywalls, keys or registration gating functionality, and no remote or obfuscated code ([Blender Code blog](https://code.blender.org/2022/10/blender-extensions-platform/)). It runs a **public approval queue** with statuses such as "awaiting changes", lists themes as a first-class category, keeps abuse reports visible to moderators only, and says reviews are "not meant as a bug tracker" ([approval queue](https://extensions.blender.org/approval-queue); [DevTalk](https://devtalk.blender.org/t/extensions-platform/34663?page=19)). Figma Community adds two ideas: an **interactive preview before commitment**, and **remix lineage** with creator profiles ([Figma Help](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/360040035974--Beta-Publish-a-File-to-your-Profile)). For djmanzo that means rendering each skin onto a mock deck and playing each visual loop on its item page. Obsidian now scans every plugin version and shows a **safety scorecard** ([Obsidian Help](https://obsidian.md/help/plugin-security)). Badges alone are weak, as VS Code shows: researchers obtained a "verified" badge for a typosquatted theme by buying a domain ([Cybersecurity News](https://cybersecuritynews.com/flaws-vs-code-marketplace-malicious-extensions)). The scorecard should show review status, data-only checks, licence and date, and skins should stay data-only. For tips, itch.io and Gumroad prove pay-what-you-want with a zero minimum works ([itch.io docs](https://itch.io/docs); [Gumroad Help](https://gumroad.com/help/article/133-pay-what-you-want-pricing)). Linking out to each creator's own Ko-fi or Liberapay page keeps djmanzo from handling third parties' money. That last point is an operational inference, not sourced law.

The forum's trust levels should follow Discourse, the best-documented model. It sandboxes newcomers (TL0 posts carry at most one image and two links) and unlocks TL1 by **reading: 5 topics, 30 posts and 10 minutes**, because reading is "the most fundamental and healthy action in any community". It also lets regulars' spam flags hide a newcomer's post instantly ([Discourse blog](https://blog.discourse.org/2018/06/understanding-discourse-trust-levels/)). Discourse itself says the TL3 rule of visiting on 50% of days is often too high, so a small forum should lower it. Healthy communities also publish a friendly "why can't I do X yet?" page ([Fairphone forum](https://forum.fairphone.com/t/forum-user-access-levels-explained/665)). For the wishlist, statuses are converging on **Under review → Planned → In progress → Complete, with an honest "Not on the roadmap"** ([ClickUp](https://feedback.clickup.com/changelog/new-improved-feature-request-statuses); [Canny](https://intercom.help/canny/en/articles/3828148-public-roadmap)). Public voting amplifies a vocal minority, snowballs early leaders and records chosen solutions rather than underlying problems ([Savio](https://www.savio.io/blog/feature-voting); [ProdPad](https://www.prodpad.com/blog/feedback-voting-bias-in-product-decisions/)). The fix is to sort by new or trending by default, merge duplicates visibly, ask "what were you trying to do?", and link every shipped item to its release note.

The island already gives events a place, and Club Penguin's rule gives them a calendar. It held **about one party a month that redecorated the central hubs** while the edges stayed calm, and most parties gave away at least one free item ([Club Penguin Wiki](https://clubpenguin.fandom.com/wiki/Portal:Parties), a fan source). Habbo showed the value of community-run events ([hyperPad](https://www.hyperpad.com/blog/lessons-from-habbo-hotel)). Competitive sale games are the cautionary case. Steam's *Saliens* (2018) was widely scripted, and large groups "kinda just win by default" ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/players-are-cheating-the-steam-summer-sale-game-but-th-1827062509)), while Steam's later collectible stickers proved more durable ([GameStar](https://www.gamestar.de/artikel/steam-lunar-sale-2025-uebersicht,3426675.html)). djmanzo should therefore decorate the plaza seasonally, give a free keepsake for visiting, prefer a collect-and-browse "passport" to team races, and hold a "You asked, we built" wall.

### EU rules make honest offers the only legal offers

Several EU rules shape special offers and moderation:

| Rule | What it requires | What it means in the interface |
|---|---|---|
| UCPD Annex I, point 7 | Bans falsely stating that an offer is available only for a very limited time. Timers that reset on refresh, or offers that outlive their timer, are deceptive ([Maastricht University Press](https://pubpub.maastrichtuniversitypress.nl/pub/chapter-6-dark-patterns-as-infringements-of-eu-consumer-law)) | A server-enforced end date written in words; the price really reverts |
| Price Indication Directive, Art. 6a | Every announced reduction must show the **lowest price of the prior 30 days**; the CJEU confirmed the strict reading in *Aldi Süd*, C‑330/23 ([HSF Kramer](https://www.hsfkramer.com/notes/ip/2024-posts/cjeu-confirms-that-price-reduction-claims-must-be-based-on-lowest-price-in-last-30-days)) | "Lowest price in last 30 days: €N" beside any "−X%"; standing student or regional tiers fall outside it (confirm with counsel) |
| DSA Art. 25 | Platforms may not design interfaces that deceive or manipulate, covering nagging and cancellation harder than sign-up ([Springlex](https://www.springlex.eu/en/packages/dsa/dsa-regulation/article-25/)) | No confirmshaming, no pre-ticked extras, no modals that reappear after dismissal |
| DSA Art. 16 | Every hosting service, of any size, needs an easy electronic notice mechanism and must inform both parties of decisions ([Presencis](https://presencis.com/regulations/dsa/article-16/)) | A "Report" control on every item, post and profile; acknowledgement and decision to the reporter; reasons sent to the uploader |
| DSA Arts. 15 and 19–28 | Micro and small enterprises are exempt from transparency reports and the heavier platform duties ([sota.io](https://sota.io/blog/eu-digital-services-act-2024-hosting-provider-developer-guide)) | No appeals bureaucracy required |

The 2022 EU sweep found fake countdowns on **42 of 399 retail sites** ([Maastricht University Press](https://pubpub.maastrichtuniversitypress.nl/pub/chapter-6-dark-patterns-as-infringements-of-eu-consumer-law)), so these rules are enforced against real practice. Two points remain uncertain. Whether Art. 25 binds a micro enterprise is unclear, because one source places the exemption in Art. 19 ([Presencis](https://presencis.com/regulations/dsa/article-25/)); the UCPD binds regardless. The **Digital Fairness Act**, which targets countdown timers, addictive design and in-game currencies, was expected around Q4 2026, and no adopted text was found ([Osborne Clarke](https://www.osborneclarke.com/insights/digital-fairness-act-unpacked-dark-patterns)). This is not legal advice, and the Commission's guidance notices were read only through law-firm summaries.

## Conclusion

Two findings that look separate point the same way. The award jury, NN/g's test participants, and EU consumer law all penalise the same thing: spectacle or pressure standing between a person and their task. For djmanzo that means the brand's best differentiator is not graphical ambition, which Bruno Simon and abeto will always out-spend. It is a combination nobody in DJ software currently offers: teaching that you can touch and that sounds good on the first try, honest gates and honest prices, and a community that feels like a calm plaza rather than a sale. The Camelot dial and the beatmatching toy are the most defensible pieces of original content in the whole plan, because the notes found nothing equivalent on the web.

Three decisions carry most of the risk. The first is the painter's TSL port, which stands between the brand's look and WebGPU. The second is the discipline to keep a plain HTML path for shop, forum and wishlist even when the island is finished. The third is provenance: a manifest recording the licence, model and plan behind every image, track and 3D prop. None of the research can show that any of this *sounds* or *feels* right on a real phone. Load time, frame rate and whether the first scratch delights a stranger can only be settled by putting a mid-range Android device and a non-DJ relative in front of it, which is the test *Messenger*'s makers said mattered most.
